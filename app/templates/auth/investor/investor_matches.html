🧠 How AI Matching Works
1
Semantic Embedding (60%) — Your investment thesis, sector expertise, and portfolio profile are converted into a 384-dimensional vector using all-MiniLM-L6-v2. Each startup's pitch deck is embedded the same way. Cosine similarity measures conceptual alignment.
2
Stage Alignment (20%) — Exact stage match scores 1.0, adjacent stage (e.g. Seed vs Pre-Seed) scores 0.6, distant stages score 0.
3
Funding Fit (10%) — Startup's funding ask vs your min–max ticket range. Within range = 1.0, within 2× = 0.5, out of range = 0.
4
Engagement Signal (10%) — Normalised profile views act as a traction proxy. High-traction startups get a mild boost.
